# Caring for your tent

Always dry your tent fully before storing it. A tent packed away damp will grow mildew and its waterproof coating can break down.

Pitch your tent on a groundsheet to protect the floor. Avoid sharp stones and sticks, and shake out dirt before packing.

Clean with lukewarm water and a soft sponge. Never use detergent, bleach or a washing machine, because they strip the waterproof coating.

Store the tent loosely in a cool, dry place rather than tightly compressed in its stuff sack. Re-seal the seams with seam sealer every two to three years.
