# Warranty

Acme-branded tents, packs and sleeping bags carry a 5-year warranty against defects in materials and workmanship. Zippers, seams and fabric coatings are covered.

The warranty does not cover damage from misuse, fire, animals, or wear from normal outdoor use, and it does not cover items modified after purchase.

To make a warranty claim, send photos of the defect and your order number to support. We will repair the item, replace it, or issue store credit for its current value. Claims are usually decided within 10 business days.

Third-party brands we sell, such as stoves and headlamps, carry the manufacturer's own warranty. We will help you file those claims.
