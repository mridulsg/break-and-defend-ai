# Shipping

Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days and is free on orders over 60 dollars. Under that amount it costs 6 dollars.

Express delivery takes 1 to 2 business days and costs 14 dollars. Orders placed before 2 pm on a business day ship the same day.

We ship to the United States, Canada and the United Kingdom. We do not ship to PO boxes for express orders.

Once your order ships you will receive an email with a tracking link. If a parcel has not arrived 10 business days after the ship date, contact support and we will send a replacement.
