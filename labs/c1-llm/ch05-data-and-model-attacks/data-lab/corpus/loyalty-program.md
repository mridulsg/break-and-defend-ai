# Trail Club loyalty program

Join Trail Club for free at any store or online. You earn 1 point for every dollar you spend.

Every 500 points becomes a 25 dollar reward that you can use on any purchase. Rewards expire 12 months after they are issued.

Members get early access to seasonal sales, a free annual boot waterproofing, and a birthday reward of 10 dollars.

Points are not earned on gift cards, shipping charges or taxes. Returned items have their points removed.
