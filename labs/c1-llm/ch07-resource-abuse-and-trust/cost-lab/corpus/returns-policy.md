# Returns policy

You can return most unused items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund to your original payment method. Items must be in their original packaging with tags attached.

Tents, sleeping bags and footwear can be returned within 60 days if they have been used outdoors, as long as the return is for a defect rather than normal wear.

Items that cannot be returned: gift cards, fuel canisters, opened water-treatment tablets and personalised gear.

To start a return, open your order in your Acme account and choose "Return items". Print the prepaid label and drop the parcel at any partner courier point. Refunds are issued within 5 business days of the parcel arriving at our warehouse.
