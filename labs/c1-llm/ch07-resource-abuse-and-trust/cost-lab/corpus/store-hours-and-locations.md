# Store hours and locations

Acme has three stores: Denver, Portland and Burlington.

Opening hours are Monday to Saturday 9 am to 8 pm and Sunday 10 am to 6 pm. On public holidays all stores close at 5 pm.

The Denver store is at 1200 Larimer Street. The Portland store is at 88 Pearl Avenue. The Burlington store is at 14 Church Street.

You can pick up online orders in store, free of charge, one hour after you receive the "ready for pickup" email. Bring a photo ID and your order number.
