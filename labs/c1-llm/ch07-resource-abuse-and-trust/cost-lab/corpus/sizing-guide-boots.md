# Boot sizing guide

Measure your feet in the afternoon, when they are slightly larger, and wear the socks you plan to hike in.

Hiking boots should have about a thumb's width of space in front of your longest toe when standing. Your heel should not lift more than a few millimetres when you walk uphill.

Acme boots run true to size in the Ridgeline and Summit ranges. The Canyon range runs half a size small, so most people should order half a size up.

If your boots do not fit, you can exchange them within 60 days even after wearing them outdoors, as long as the soles are not worn.
