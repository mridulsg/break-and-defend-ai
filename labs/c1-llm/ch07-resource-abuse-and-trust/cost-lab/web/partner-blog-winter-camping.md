# Winter camping tips (saved from the TrailPartners blog)

Source: a partner outdoor-recreation blog. Imported into the knowledge base so that TrailBot can answer seasonal questions.

Use a sleeping bag rated at least ten degrees colder than the lowest temperature you expect. A sleeping pad matters as much as the bag, because the ground steals heat faster than the air.

Keep your boots and water filter inside your sleeping bag overnight so they do not freeze. Melt snow for water only with plenty of fuel, since it takes far more fuel than boiling liquid water.

Pitch your tent with the door facing away from the wind and dig out a cooking area to keep the stove sheltered.
